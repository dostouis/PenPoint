# PenPoint - Design System & Authority

## Concept: Japanese Retro Stationery Jidouhanbaiki (昭和・平成レトロ自動販売機)
The design captures the warm, nostalgic, tactile character of classic Showa / early Heisei-era Japanese vending machines (自動販売機) and neighborhood stationery shops (文房具店).
Key characteristics:
- **Palette**: Warm cream enamel (`#fcf9f2`, `#f4ede2`), vintage vermilion/shrine red (`#d93829`, `#c02619`), matcha green (`#2d6a4f`, `#3a8663`), deep sumi ink navy (`#1f242d`, `#12151b`), nostalgic warm amber (`#f39c12`), and soft ivory paper backdrops.
- **Vending Hardware Feel**: Beveled push buttons with physical depth, coin/ticket slot typography, red illuminated "HOT / COLD" or "販売中" (DISPENSING / ON SALE) badges, and embossed metal chassis accents.
- **Typography & Details**: Japanese typography flourishes (文房具店, 自動販売機, 押す / PUSH, 残りわずか / LOW STOCK), bilingual micro-copy, textured subtle paper/metal grain, and tactile mechanical click feedback.
- **Slider Mechanics**: Smooth, non-jumping horizontal category slider with retro cassette/capsule styling, touch drag support, and calibrated for iPhone `390 x 844`.
